A little about me

Hello, I’m Kamila.

I believe yoga can be a generous pause in a busy day: a chance to listen, find steadiness, and make room for whatever you bring to the mat.

My practice is always growing, and this space is where I share a little of that journey. Whether you’re brand new or finding your way back, you’re welcome here.
